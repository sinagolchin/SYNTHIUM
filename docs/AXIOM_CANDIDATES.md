# AXIOM CANDIDATES — Sina's three, formalized and audited

Standard applied (the project's own): an axiom must be able to FAIL. If nothing
could count against it, it is a definition or a value, not an axiom. Each entry
below gets: statement → what kind of object it is → testable form → failure
conditions → audit against existing data → required instruments.

---

## A1 — Trajectory: "Every being's trajectory is toward more enjoyment."

**Kind:** empirical claim about agents (descriptive).

**Circularity audit — FAILS AS WRITTEN.** The clause "subject to defining
enjoyment" is the leak-hole: if enjoyment is defined from observed choices, the
axiom is true by construction (whatever was chosen = "enjoyment"). This is the
E-unit disease at the level of ethics: reading the measure off the outcome.

**Repair (mandatory):** define an enjoyment measure FIRST, independent of the
choices it will be tested on. Only then is the claim live:
    A1': For measure H fixed in advance, dH/dt ≥ 0 along chosen trajectories,
         statistically, absent constraint.

**Failure conditions (real, expected):** addiction (H falls, behavior persists),
chosen suffering (askesis, grief kept deliberately), self-sacrifice, exploration
(accepting low H now for possible high H later — which may RESCUE the axiom if H
is expected/long-horizon rather than instantaneous; that choice of horizon must
be fixed in advance, not after seeing the data).

**Verdict:** genuine axiom candidate, BLOCKED pending its own ruler (an H
measure). This is a second Synthium domain: build the enjoyment ruler the way the
judicial ruler was built — propose units, commit blind, let reality edit.

---

## A2 — Non-interference: "The way to reach enjoyment should not interfere with others' enjoyment."

**Kind audit — CATEGORY ERROR AS WRITTEN.** "Should" makes it a norm; reality
violates it hourly, so as description it is dead on arrival. It must choose a job:

- **A2-value (chosen, not tested):** part of the DEFINITION of fairness the
  project aims at. Legitimate, but then it is the target, not an axiom.
- **A2-empirical (testable now):** "Adjudication systems detect and price
  interference: when one agent's enjoyment-seeking measurably reduces another's,
  systems impose a transfer/penalty scaling with the interference."

**Failure conditions for A2-empirical:** legal systems that systematically ignore
large interferences (pollution long unpriced — partial falsifier, note it), or
punish non-interfering enjoyment (victimless-crime laws — a real strain on the
claim; both logged as expected friction, not excuses).

**Audit against our data:** the 130+ logged cases are almost all priced
interference — torts (injury), contract breach (defeated reliance), nuisance,
zoning, discrimination. Courts are, descriptively, interference-pricing machines.
A2-empirical is CONSISTENT with everything gathered so far, untested in its
scaling claim (penalty ∝ interference) — that scaling is measurable from damages
data and is the natural next quantitative test.

**Verdict:** as written, not an axiom. Split into A2-value (adopted as target if
Sina says so) + A2-empirical (live, testable on existing connectors).

---

## A3 — Preservation floor: "Nothing is more valuable than preserving the life of the being/system."

**Kind:** strict priority claim (lexicographic: survival ≻ everything).

**Audit against reality — FALSIFIED at the individual level.** Martyrs, rescuers,
parents, soldiers, salmon; humans routinely price things above their own
survival. **Escape-hatch warning:** redefining "the being" after each
counterexample (it was "really" the family/genes/cause being preserved) is the
same unfalsifiable move as A1's floating "enjoyment." Forbidden. If system-level
preservation is meant, the system must be named IN ADVANCE.

**Honest re-scopings that are live:**
- A3-system: "self-maintaining systems act to preserve themselves" — near
  Spinoza's conatus / autopoiesis; testable per named system; known exceptions
  (apoptosis, planned obsolescence of institutions) become structure.
- A3-legal (testable NOW): "legal systems rank life at/near the top of the value
  hierarchy." Evidence for: homicide is everywhere the apex crime; necessity
  rarely justifies killing; life interests trump property in remedies doctrine.
  The EXCEPTIONS are the payload: self-defense, war, capital punishment are
  exactly where a society prices something above a life. Mapping those exceptions
  = measuring a jurisdiction's real value hierarchy. That is a ruler.

**Verdict:** false as universal; strong as A3-legal; A3-system possible with the
system named in advance.

---

## Consistency audit of the SET

1. **A1 vs A3 conflict:** beings demonstrably risk life FOR enjoyment
   (mountaineers; much of human history). A strict A3 floor contradicts an
   unconstrained A1 climb. The set therefore REQUIRES either an ordering or an
   exchange rate between survival-probability and enjoyment. That the axioms
   force this question is evidence they are doing real work.
2. **Joint structure:** A1 = objective (climb H), A2 = constraint (no crossing
   others' H), A3 = floor (state must stay alive). Objective + constraint + floor
   is exactly a well-posed constrained-optimization problem:
       maximize H_i  subject to  interference_ij ≤ 0  and  survival_i ≥ floor.
3. **Bridge to the judicial domain:** courts adjudicate precisely the violations
   of A2 and A3 — a tort is an A2 violation priced in money; a homicide case is
   an A3 violation priced in years. The judicial data is the FRICTION SURFACE of
   this axiom set. The two workstreams are one project.

# ⭐ A0 — THE EPISTEMIC ACCESS AXIOM (candidate, proposed attempt 30)

> **A reviewing court may revise only what it is positioned to know at least as well
> as the court below.**

Unlike A1-A3 (Sina's ethics axioms, which are normative), A0 is **structural**: it
claims to be the ground from which the judicial regularities we measured all follow.

## What it derives (each of these was MEASURED before A0 was written)
1. **The seam.** An appellate court cannot see the witnesses; it CAN read the statute.
   So facts are armored and law moves. ✓ (all 250 committed cases)
2. **Level-invariance of the fact channel** (attempt 29's surprise). Climbing the
   hierarchy gives you *zero* extra access to a witness's demeanor — a supreme court
   justice sits no closer to the witness stand than an appellate judge. So the fact
   rate CANNOT improve with level. But climbing DOES improve your position on law
   (harmonizing authority, more judges, more time). Hence law-side 0.36 -> 0.51 while
   fact-side stays 0.14 -> 0.14. **A0 predicts exactly this asymmetry.** ✓
3. **Every override channel** — all four restore epistemic access:
   - clearly erroneous / no substantial evidence: the contradiction is ON PAPER, and
     paper is fully visible to the appellate court
   - procedural integrity (Weaver): the finder's bias is visible in the record
   - sufficiency: accepts the facts, asks a pure legal question -> full access
   - documentary contradiction: the document is as legible upstairs as downstairs
4. **The credibility floor.** Demeanor is NEVER transmissible on appeal -> absolute armor.

## The NEW prediction, and its test
A0 predicts a split *within* fact challenges that no previous version of the ruler
saw: **documentary fact challenges should WIN; credibility fact challenges should LOSE.**

Coded by what kind of evidence the disputed fact RESTS ON (readable from the facts,
not the holding):

| basis of the disputed fact | fact-findings disturbed |
|---|---|
| **DOCUMENTARY** (exhibits, records, undisputed paper) | **4/4 = 100%** |
| **CREDIBILITY** (live testimony, demeanor, expert opinion) | **0/13 = 0%** |

**Every fact reversal in the entire register is documentary. Not one credibility
finding has ever been overturned — at either court level, in any jurisdiction.**

## ⚠ THE HONEST CAVEATS — this is not yet proven
1. **CIRCULARITY RISK (serious).** Courts *say* "contradicted by the record" when they
   reverse and *say* "we defer to credibility" when they affirm. I may be reading the
   court's own justification back as if it were an independent variable. Mitigation: I
   coded from what the fact RESTS ON (invoices vs. testimony), which appears in the
   facts section. But I read whole syllabi, so contamination is possible.
   **CLEAN TEST, PRE-REGISTERED: on the next batch, commit the DOCUMENTARY/CREDIBILITY
   coding from the FACTS SECTION ONLY, before reading any holding.**
2. **SELECTION RISK.** Perhaps appellants only bring documentary fact challenges when
   they are strong, and toss in credibility challenges as makeweights. That alone could
   produce this split with A0 false. Needs appellant-brief-level data we do not have.
3. **NEAR-TAUTOLOGY?** A0 is close to what courts say about themselves ("credibility is
   for the trier of fact"). Is it therefore empty? **No — and this is the point of an
   axiom.** Its value is not novelty but (a) UNIFICATION: one principle generates the
   seam, level-invariance, all four override channels, and the sufficiency distinction;
   (b) PORTABILITY: it predicts the same structure in France, Germany, Canada — and we
   found it there, in their own doctrinal language; (c) FALSIFIABILITY: see below.
4. **POST-HOC.** A0 was written after seeing level-invariance. The documentary/credibility
   test used data already collected — a retrodiction, not a prospective test. The
   prospective test is OWED.

## How to KILL A0 (the falsifier, stated plainly)
**Find an appellate court overturning a pure credibility finding — where no document
contradicts it, no procedural defect exists, and the record is merely re-weighed.**
A0 says this should be vanishingly rare. Currently: 0 in 13. If such cases turn up at
any material rate, A0 is wrong.

## Relation to Sina's axioms
A0 is an *institutional* non-interference principle — structurally the same shape as
A2 (do not interfere where you have no standing to). A2 says: do not cross into
another's domain. A0 says: do not revise what you cannot see better. The ethics axioms
and the judicial data may share a common form: **authority is bounded by access.**
Speculative; flagged as such.

## A0 — ADVERSARIAL TEST (attempt 31): the hunt for the falsifier FAILED

Method: rather than gather more confirmations, I searched *specifically* for the case
that would kill A0 — an appellate court overturning a pure credibility finding — using
queries built to surface exactly that outcome:
- "credibility determination clearly erroneous reversed new trial ordered"
- "court improperly credited testimony witness not worthy of belief reversed"

**Result: zero credibility reversals found.**

The hunt DID surface a fact reversal — **Rose v. Commissioner of Correction** (CT
Supreme, 2023), where the Appellate Court was reversed because the habeas court's
finding was "clearly erroneous." A0 would die here if the finding were credibility-
based. It is not: the habeas court read a **TRANSCRIPT** as saying counsel advised
refiling "now"; the Supreme Court read the **same transcript** and found it said no
such thing, and that both witnesses testified consistently that no deadline was ever
given. **Paper, fully visible upstairs. A0 predicted it.**

**Komisarjevsky** (the Cheshire home-invasion case) is the sharpest confirmation yet.
The trial court found the witness **credible** — and *still* found the defendant had
failed to prove a police e-mail existed, because she had deleted it and lost the only
printout. The Supreme Court deferred, finding no clear error. **A0's reading: the
document was GONE. Nobody upstairs could see it. So the finding stands.** Access, not
credibility, is doing the work — exactly as the axiom says.

### Updated channel tally (n=19 fact challenges, 2 US states + 5 foreign systems)
| basis of the disputed fact | disturbed |
|---|---|
| **DOCUMENTARY** | **5/5 = 100%** |
| **CREDIBILITY** | **0/14 = 0%** |

### Honest weight of this result
- A survived falsification attempt is worth more than a dozen confirmations. This was
  a real attempt: the queries were designed to find the killer case, not to avoid it.
- **BUT: absence of evidence is not proof.** The search ranks by relevance, not by
  outcome; such cases could exist and not surface. The claim is "I hunted and did not
  find one," not "they do not exist."
- The circularity caveat from attempt 30 STANDS and is not resolved by this test.
- Status: **A0 is not proven. It is UNFALSIFIED after a deliberate attempt to falsify
  it** — which is the strongest status any of our claims has ever held.

# THE PROPOSED AXIOM SET (attempt 32) — three structural axioms, and a factorization

Claude's proposals, derived from 263 committed cases. These are STRUCTURAL axioms
(about how judgment works), distinct from Sina's A1-A3 which are NORMATIVE (about
what is good). See "Two Skeletons" below.

## ⭐ A0 — EPISTEMIC ACCESS  [status: unfalsified after adversarial attack]
> **A reviewing court may revise only what it is positioned to know at least as well
> as the court below.**

Derives: the fact/law seam; the level-invariance of the fact channel; all four
override channels; the credibility floor; the sufficiency/fact distinction.
Evidence: documentary fact challenges 5/5 disturbed; credibility 0/14. Survived a
deliberate hunt for its falsifier (attempt 31).

## ⭐ A1 — CHANNEL EXCLUSIVITY  [NEW — and A0 cannot explain what it explains]
> **Every question has a proper forum and a proper moment. A court will not decide a
> question presented in the wrong forum or at the wrong time — however meritorious.**

**Why this must be a SEPARATE axiom.** Ohio's apex court denies original-action writs
0-for-9 (0-for-14 pooled). Every one of those cases is coded **pure_law** — meaning
the court had *complete* epistemic access to the issue. A0 grants full permission to
act, and the court refuses anyway. **Something independent of access is gating them.**
That something is channel.

The four faces of A1 (all measured):
- **wrong forum**: mandamus denied for "adequate remedy at law" (Zander, Evans, White,
  Smith, Miller, Cobb, Roush, O'Malley, Yeager, Mitchell, McCarthy...) — 0 moved
- **too early**: no final judgment (Office Condo v. Rompre; Clinton v. Aspinwall)
- **too late**: procedural default (Lewis v. Comm'r); unpreserved claim declined
  (Lueders' Miranda claim — "record inadequate for review")
- **already spent**: res judicata (Kendrick, Johnson); Rechtskraft (OLG Hamm)

A1 DERIVES the v3 dismissal split (gatekeeping 0/9 vs merits 3/3) that we discovered
empirically two weeks before this axiom was written.

**A1's sharp, falsifiable prediction: CHANNEL BEATS MERIT.** A litigant who is RIGHT
on the substance but in the wrong channel still loses. This is counterintuitive — a
"justice" theory of courts predicts the opposite. Current data: 0/9. **To kill A1:
find a court reaching the merits of a claim brought in a plainly improper channel
because the claim was strong.**

## A2 — INERTIA (PRESUMPTION OF REGULARITY)  [near-tautological, but foundational]
> **A judgment stands unless error is demonstrated. The burden never shifts to the
> judgment.**

This is why the always-affirm baseline is ~62% and why it is so hard to beat. It is
close to a restatement of the burden of proof — but it is the axiom that *makes
affirmance the ground state*. Without it there is no baseline to beat.

## ⭐ THE FACTORIZATION (the structural claim that ties them together)
If A0, A1, A2 are INDEPENDENT gates, a case moves only if it passes all three:

  **P(move) = P(error shown | A2) x P(court can see it | A0) x P(right channel | A1)**

Independent multiplicative gates => **additive in log-odds**. That is exactly the form
our fitted model took, and it was never imposed — it was fit.

**Tested (attempt 32):** added a lawside x apex interaction term to the fit.
  additive model: logL = -98.71 | with interaction: logL = -98.69
  likelihood-ratio = 0.04 (chi-sq, 1 df; 3.84 needed for p<.05); coefficient +0.21
**No significant interaction. The gates behave independently.** The empirical model's
three features are the three axioms' shadows:
  A0 -> the LAYER feature | A1 -> the POSTURE/dismissal-subtype feature | A2 -> the INTERCEPT

⚠ **Honest limit:** a null interaction result at n=151 has LOW POWER. This is
"consistent with independence," NOT "independence proven." A larger register could
still reveal coupling.

## TWO SKELETONS (why Sina's axioms haven't shown up in the data)
A legal system has at least two structures:
- the **EPISTEMIC/PROCEDURAL skeleton** — who may revise what, from where, when.
  *This is what A0/A1/A2 describe, and what all 263 cases measured.*
- the **VALUE skeleton** — what a harm is worth. What life costs. What freedom costs.
  *Sina's A1-A3 (enjoyment-trajectory, non-interference, preservation-floor) live HERE.*

**The instrument has been pointed at the first skeleton the whole time.** That is why
the ethics axioms produced no signal — not because they are wrong, but because we were
not measuring their layer. The structural axioms are VALUE-NEUTRAL: an unjust legal
system would obey A0/A1/A2 exactly as a just one does (apartheid courts had the seam;
so did the German Revision structure we blind-tested). **The seam proves courts serve
KNOWLEDGE, not justice.** That is an important and slightly uncomfortable finding.

To test the value skeleton we must measure PRICES, not procedures: sentences vs harm,
damages vs interference, and A3's exception-map (self-defense / war / capital punishment
= the places a society ranked life below something else). Untested. A real second domain.

## T1 — Review barrier (thermodynamic form). Added 2026-11-10, PRE-REGISTERED before any data is coded for it.

**Origin:** Sina's question (2026-11-10): can the rulers take thermodynamic form, with
subject-specific parameters and constants? Proposed by Claude from the pattern in the
15-row ledger (every movement law-side; fact attacks held). That pattern MOTIVATED T1, so
those 15 rows may not be used to test it.

**Statement:** an appellate challenge must cross a barrier set by its standard of review.
    P(move | challenge) = A_j · exp( −B(s) / T_j )
  - s = standard of review applied to the challenged point (ordinal: de novo < mixed /
    reasonableness < abuse of discretion / clearly erroneous / palpable and overriding /
    substantial evidence < jury verdict / credibility finding).
  - B(s) = barrier height. UNIVERSAL ("physics"): same ordering and same relative spacing in
    every jurisdiction.
  - T_j = jurisdiction "temperature"; A_j = attempt rate. LOCAL ("ethics"): fitted per
    jurisdiction.
  - "move" = notch ≠ CLEAN_AFFIRM on that challenged point.

**Kind:** empirical, quantitative, falsifiable.

**Unit of analysis:** the individual challenged point (ground of appeal), not the case,
because one opinion can hold a fact point and move a law point (Goncalves).

**Failure conditions (decided now, not after the data):**
  1. Ordering fails: in any jurisdiction with ≥ 30 coded points, a higher-barrier class moves
     more often than a lower one beyond its 95% interval.
  2. Universality fails: when B(s) is fitted separately per jurisdiction, the spacing differs
     beyond its intervals in ≥ 2 of the jurisdictions with enough data. (Then the barrier is
     "ethics", not "physics".)
  3. Form fails: a plain step model (law moves, facts don't) fits as well (ΔAIC < 2). Then the
     exponential adds nothing and T1 collapses into the existing law/fact pattern.
  4. Rival wins: party power (Greene rival) explains movement better than s, with s held fixed.

**Data needed:** per challenged point: jurisdiction, court tier, s, layer, moved yes/no,
party-power proxy (state vs individual; represented vs pro se; corporate vs natural person).
Target ≥ 30 points per jurisdiction in ≥ 5 jurisdictions before any fit. Coded blind where
the tool allows.

**Known confounds:** selection (parties appeal weak fact points less, or apex courts only
take law questions); a non-random sample of cases; s is sometimes contested within the
opinion itself.

**Status:** PROPOSED, pre-registered, no data. Do not fit before the target volume.

## DRAFT (not adopted) — G1, the power-dynamics rival to T1 ("Greene rival"). Drafted by Claude 2026-11-12 after Campaign A closed; for Sina's review. Changes no axiom or coding rule.

**Claim to beat T1 with:** what moves a case on appeal is not the height of the review barrier
but who is pushing against it. Power here means the ordinary, measurable advantages of a party:
being a repeat player (the state, an insurer, a university, a utility), having counsel when the
other side is pro se, controlling the record, and being the party the forum serves. On this view the
standard of review is partly a tool power uses: the strong party frames its loss as a legal error
(low barrier) and the weak party is left attacking findings (high barrier). If that is right, the
law/fact pattern T1 explains would be a shadow of party power, not a property of review.

**Form:** P(move) = A_j · exp( −B(s)/T_j ) · exp( γ·Δpower ), with Δpower the appellant's power
minus the appellee's. G1 wins (T1 failure condition 4) if, with s held fixed, γ is clearly non-zero
and adding Δpower improves fit more than adding s does to a power-only model.

**What G1 predicts that T1 does not:** (1) within one standard of review, organisations and the
state win reversal more often as appellants and lose it less often as appellees than individuals do;
(2) pro se appellants lose more often even on de novo points; (3) the share of points a party frames
as law rises with its power.

**Data it needs:** the current party_power_proxy is symmetric (individual_v_organization does not
say who appealed). G1 needs a directional proxy: appellant type, appellee type, and represented vs pro se
for each side. That is a coding-rule change, so it is filed as proposal P2 and not applied.

**Status:** DRAFT, untested, no fit. Same volume rule as T1 (≥30 points in ≥5 jurisdictions).
