# Cross-border test — does the ruler travel? (first probe, via Legal Data Hunter)

The ruler's core claim: appellate movement lives in the LAW layer, not the FACT
layer. If that is real (not a Connecticut quirk), it should recur in other legal
systems. Legal Data Hunter reaches 200+ jurisdictions, including civil-law
systems that work very differently from US common law. First probe:

## Prediction (committed before looking)
Civil-law countries split appeals into two layers. The ruler should SPLIT with
them: nearly break at the fact-reviewing layer, hold unusually strongly at the
law-only layer.

## What the cases show
- **France — Cour de cassation** (top court): reviews *moyens* (legal grounds),
  anchors each ruling in a Code article, and when it overturns it "casse" then
  "renvoi" — sends the case back for the facts to be redone elsewhere. It is a
  *juge du droit*: forbidden by design from touching facts.
- **Germany — Revision (BGH / BVerwG)**: German procedure explicitly separates
  *Berufung* (re-hears fact + law) from *Revision* (legal error only; the trial
  court's fact-findings bind the court). The pulled cases are all Revision.

## Honest reading
The fact/law split is not a common-law accident — it is a recurring ARCHITECTURE
of high courts across legal families and languages (US, France, Germany). BUT the
strength has two regimes, and honesty requires separating them:

1. **Supreme / cassation courts** (US Supreme, FR Cassation, DE Revision): law-only
   BY DESIGN. "Only law moves" is nearly definitional here, so it confirms the
   ruler's *shape* but is not a surprising empirical win.
2. **Intermediate appeals that CAN review facts** (US state appeals — our
   Connecticut data; FR cour d'appel; DE Berufung): here facts are ALLOWED to
   move, so "facts rarely move" is a genuine empirical claim, not a definition.
   Connecticut confirmed it empirically. The real next test is whether cour
   d'appel / Berufung show the same empirical deference.

## Status of this probe
Qualitative, not yet blind-scored. It reads court STRUCTURE from case snippets +
known procedure, not a quantified prediction batch like the 119 CT cases. So it
is strong evidence the ruler's distinction is REAL and cross-systemic, logged as
architecture — with the quantified foreign test (intermediate fact-reviewing
courts, blind-scored) as the next job.

## Why this matters for the thesis
It supports "if the ruler is real, it appears everywhere" — the fact/law seam is
visible in three legal systems on two legal traditions in three languages. It
also sharpens the fairness aim: a court that moves a FACT layer it is structurally
not supposed to touch is exactly an outlier the ruler would flag.

## Fifth system: India — the seam is written into the STATUTE BOOK

India (common-law family, but heavily codified) makes the fact/law boundary
explicit in legislation, across categories:

- **Civil — Section 100, Code of Civil Procedure:** a Second Appeal to the High
  Court lies ONLY on "a substantial question of LAW." Concurrent findings of fact
  by the two courts below are binding and cannot be reopened. (Our pulled cases
  are exactly these "Regular Second Appeals" / RSA.)
- **Criminal — Section 378, Code of Criminal Procedure:** appeals against an
  ACQUITTAL are subject to a heavy-deference rule — a higher court may reverse a
  fact-based acquittal only if the trial view was "perverse"/impossible. Section
  401 similarly limits revisional re-examination of facts. (Our pulled cases —
  Geeta Devi, H.D. Sundara, Joseph Stephen — are precisely these.)
- **Administrative — Articles 226/32 judicial review:** courts review the
  legality and process of a decision (LAW), not its merits (FACT) — the same
  seam, under the Wednesbury/proportionality standard.

So in India the ruler's core distinction is not merely a pattern courts follow —
it is enacted law, for criminal, civil, and administrative alike.

## Sixth system: Brazil — the seam as a named binding rule (Súmula 7)

Brazil (civil law, Portuguese, South America) encodes the seam as a binding
precedent of its Superior Court (STJ):

- **Súmula 7/STJ:** "a pretensão de simples reexame de prova não enseja recurso
  especial" — a special appeal CANNOT re-examine facts/evidence; only questions of
  law. Nearly every pulled STJ case (paternity, consumer, improbity) is decided by
  invoking Súmula 7 to refuse fact re-examination.
- **Two-tier structure, visible directly:** state-court *apelação* (tier 2)
  re-reviews facts freely; the STJ's *recurso especial* (tier 1) is law-only. Same
  split as France (appel vs cassation) and Germany (Berufung vs Revision).

## Sharper finding: it's not just "facts are protected" — it's a recurring TWO-TIER ARCHITECTURE

Across France, Germany, India, and Brazil the SAME design recurs:
1. an INTERMEDIATE appeal that CAN re-examine facts, and
2. an APEX appeal restricted BY RULE to law only.

The ruler's fact/law seam is the boundary BETWEEN those two tiers. This is a
stronger claim than "courts defer to facts": legal systems independently evolved
the same two-layer machine, with the fact/law line as the gate between layers.

## Seventh system: China — the first BEND (and why it's the best data point)

China (socialist civil-law tradition, Chinese, a genuinely different lineage) does
NOT share the Western two-tier design — and that is exactly what makes it useful.

**Structural divergence (the seam, as court DESIGN, does NOT hold):**
- Chinese second-instance appeal is a COMPREHENSIVE review (全面审查) of BOTH fact
  and law together — the appellate court is fully empowered to re-examine facts.
  There is no cassation-style, law-only apex tier walling facts off. (The Supreme
  People's Court issues 指导案例 "Guiding Cases" on legal rules, but that is
  guidance, not a jurisdictional bar on fact review.)
- So the DEFINITIONAL version of the seam — "the high court is forbidden from
  touching facts" — is a feature of the Western traditions (US/UK/FR/DE/IN/BR),
  NOT a human universal. China breaks it. Important, honest, and expected of a
  truly different system.

**Empirical hint (the seam, as BEHAVIOR, may still appear):**
- The one second-instance CRIMINAL case pulled (intentional injury/theft) states:
  the original judgment's finding of facts is "clear, evidence reliable and
  sufficient"; appellant submitted no new evidence; "this court CONFIRMS the facts
  and evidence found by the original judgment." I.e., a court that is FREE to
  re-decide facts still deferred to the trial findings.

**Why this is the most valuable cross-border result so far:**
It SEPARATES two claims that the six prior systems conflated:
  (a) fact/law seam as COURT DESIGN — Western, NOT universal (China refutes it);
  (b) trial fact-findings TEND TO SURVIVE as BEHAVIOR — possibly deeper and more
      universal, appearing even where it is not institutionally required.
China is the case that pries (a) and (b) apart. The ruler's real target may be
(b) — a behavioral regularity — with the Western two-tier structure being just
one institutional way to enforce it.

**Honest limits:** this rests on ONE Chinese second-instance snippet plus the
structural facts of Chinese procedure. One case is not a rate. Chinese
second-instance courts DO modify/reverse (改判) and order retrial (再审)
sometimes. Whether behavioral fact-deference holds in China needs a real scored
Chinese sample — now the single most informative test on the board, because it
targets (b) directly, stripped of (a).

## Scorecard — SEVEN systems
Six (US, UK/Commonwealth, France, Germany, India, Brazil) confirm the seam as
design/doctrine/statute. China (7th) breaks the DESIGN version but hints the
BEHAVIORAL version persists. That refinement is worth more than a 7th confirmation.

## Canada blind-depth attempt — the pace problem, made concrete

Tried to start a strict blind 3-per-category batch in Canada (BC). Read R. v.
Crawford (2024 BCCA 60) in full. Result: it was a PROCEDURAL chambers motion
(suspend sentence pending appeal), not a merits disposition — not scorable on the
T/P/L ruler. It did restate the seam explicitly ("factual findings... accorded
deference... absent palpable and overriding error"; sentence challenged on a
LEGAL framework error) — so: Canada structural confirmation logged; blind notch
score = not available from this case.

### The honest engineering constraint (now demonstrated, not asserted)
- US (Connecticut) has one-paragraph SYLLABI -> 9 cases/attempt can be blind-scored
  cheaply. That is why all ~140 rigorous cases are from there.
- Abroad there are NO syllabi -> each blind score = one full-judgment read, and
  some reads (like Crawford) turn out to be procedural and unscorable. So a strict
  3-per-category blind foreign batch is a MULTI-ATTEMPT effort (a few scorable
  cases per turn), not a single-attempt 9.

### The three honest ways forward (Sina picks)
- **A — Depth, cheap:** keep blind-scoring 9/attempt in US syllabus courts. Grows
  the coded dataset the MATH needs fastest. Weakness: one country.
- **B — Depth, abroad, slow:** commit several attempts to ONE jurisdiction
  (Canada/Australia easiest, English), a few scorable merits cases per turn, until
  a second country reaches Connecticut's standard. Highest scientific value.
- **C — Breadth, cheap:** more structural confirmations across jurisdictions
  (what widened the map to 11). Diminishing returns now.

For a MATHEMATICAL system, coefficients come from coded depth (A or B), not
breadth. Recommend B for one jurisdiction to prove the coefficients travel, with
A in parallel to keep volume up.

## FIRST CIVIL-LAW entry in the foreign BLIND register (attempt 24)

**Cass. Civ. 1, 4 juillet 2000, 98-10.744** (French Cour de cassation, apex).
Committed CLEAN_AFFIRM before reading. Actual: "REJETTE le pourvoi" = CLEAN_AFFIRM.
Notch ✓.

Significance: every prior foreign blind case (UK, AU, CA) was COMMON LAW, where
one could argue the seam is a shared-lineage artifact. France is civil law — no
common-law ancestry, codified system, judges as "la bouche de la loi." Yet the
Cassation disposed of this case with the seam AS ITS OPERATIVE RULE, in one line:
"l'appréciation par les juges du fond du caractère intentionnel d'une faute ...
est souveraine et échappe au contrôle de la Cour de Cassation" — the trial judges'
assessment of a fact is sovereign and escapes Cassation review. The court did not
merely happen to affirm; it affirmed BECAUSE the challenged matter was factual and
therefore outside its power. The French term of art — appréciation souveraine des
juges du fond — is the exact civil-law analogue of "clearly erroneous" / "palpable
and overriding error" / "plainly wrong." Four different legal languages, one seam.

## Germany added — 2nd civil-law system, and the sharpest seam case yet (attempt 25)

4 German cases committed blind.

**Three BGH Revisions (apex, criminal + professional-discipline)** — all committed
CLEAN_AFFIRM, all "als unbegründet verworfen" ✓✓✓. The BGH's own disposition
formula IS the seam: the judgment is rejected because review "keinen Rechtsfehler"
revealed — the court examines ONLY for legal error and cannot touch the trial
court's findings.

**OLG Hamm, 4 RVs 141/20 (intermediate)** — committed CLEAN_AFFIRM, actual
REVERSED. Notch MISS, but the most instructive case in the whole register:
**the case moved precisely BECAUSE a lower court got the seam wrong.** The
defendant had limited his appeal to sentencing, which made the guilt-phase
findings binding (in Rechtskraft erwachsen). The Landgericht then wrongly treated
the *personal/therapeutic* findings as binding TOO, and so failed to make its own
findings on them. Held: a court may ground its judgment only on findings it made
itself, except where findings have become binding. **The reversal is a legal error
ABOUT THE SCOPE OF FACT-BINDING.** The seam was not crossed — it was enforced. A
court that mishandles the fact/law boundary gets reversed for it.

This is the strongest possible form of evidence for the seam: it is not merely a
pattern in outcomes, it is a RULE THE SYSTEM POLICES. German law has a doctrine
for getting the seam wrong.

**Foreign blind tally: 9 cases across 5 systems (UK, AU, CA common law; FR, DE
civil law) — notch 7/9, fact-seam 9/9.** Two civil-law systems now confirm the
seam blind. Both notch misses (Ackland AU, OLG Hamm DE) were legal-layer
reversals that left fact-findings untouched — i.e. the misses CONFIRM the seam
while missing the notch.

## A NEW MECHANISM: the seam STACKS (attempt 26, Privy Council)

**TLM Company Ltd v Bedasie [2014] UKPC 25** (apex; from Trinidad & Tobago CoA).
Committed CLEAN_AFFIRM; appeal dismissed ✓. Fact-seam held.

But the *mechanism* is one we had not catalogued. The Board applies its
**concurrent-findings practice** (Devi v Roy [1946] AC 508): where TWO courts below
have concurrently found the same facts, the Board **declines to review the evidence
a third time at all** — this is not "we reviewed and found no error," it is a
refusal of jurisdiction over the question. Departure requires a miscarriage of
justice or a violation of a principle of law/procedure so serious that correcting
it would make the finding unsustainable.

**Implication for the ruler:** fact-armor is not a constant — it is CUMULATIVE.
Each successive court that agrees on the facts adds a layer of armor. This is a
new, testable, quantitative prediction:

  **P(fact reversal | 2 concurrent courts) << P(fact reversal | 1 court)**

We have been treating "fact layer" as a single flat coefficient. It should be a
function of how many courts have already affirmed the finding. This is the first
mechanism the data has offered for WHY the fact-reversal rate is ~1% rather than,
say, 10%: by the time a case reaches an apex court, the facts have usually been
found once and affirmed once — doubly armored.

**Testable now:** in our domestic register, do fact challenges at APEX (2 courts
below) fail more reliably than fact challenges at INTERMEDIATE (1 court below)?
Machine rows: intermediate fact-side 0/6 moved; apex fact-side 0/4 moved. Both
zero — the prediction is CONSISTENT but our n is too small to separate them
(both floors are at zero). Needs a larger fact-challenge sample; flagged as the
next quantitative target.

**Foreign blind tally: 10 cases / 5 systems — notch 8/10, fact-seam 10/10.**

# ⭐⭐⭐ ATTEMPT 38 — CHINA. THE PENDING FALSIFICATION TEST. A0 SURVIVES — AND IS STATED MORE NAKEDLY THAN ANYWHERE ELSE.

China was flagged from the start as the one system that could kill the epistemic
skeleton: its appellate courts operate under **全面审查 (comprehensive review)** — they
MAY re-examine BOTH fact and law. There is no law-only apex court. If Chinese courts
routinely re-find facts on a cold record, the seam is not universal.

**BLIND COMMIT (made before reading any Chinese case):** A0 predicts Chinese courts
revise facts ONLY where they re-open the evidence. A fact reversal by pure re-weighing
of a cold record — with no new evidence — KILLS A0.

## Half 1: the affirm side — 赵某故意伤害、盗窃案 (2nd instance, CAIL2018)
> 原判认定...事实清楚,证据确实、充分,所列证据已在一审开庭时**当庭宣读、出示并质证**。
> **二审期间,赵某未提交新的证据,本院对原判认定的事实和所列证据予以确认。**

"The evidence was read out, presented and **cross-examined in open court at first
instance**. **During the second instance the appellant submitted NO NEW EVIDENCE.
This court therefore CONFIRMS the facts found below.**"

**The court affirms the facts BECAUSE IT GAINED NO NEW ACCESS.** It does not invoke a
deference doctrine. It does not say "clearly erroneous." **It gives the access condition
itself as its reason.** Committed CLEAN_AFFIRM -> affirmed. ✓

## Half 2: the reversal side — 最高检 2025, 伤害类案件技术性证据实质审查典型案例
(SPP: *Typical Cases on Substantive Review of TECHNICAL Evidence in Injury Cases*)
> 案例二周某某故意伤害案,**对存在问题的鉴定意见启动重新鉴定**
> 案例一王某某...依据被害人损伤形态特征区分新伤与陈旧伤,**准确认定案件事实**

When Chinese authorities DO revise a fact, they **RE-OPEN THE EVIDENCE** — 重新鉴定
(re-run the forensic appraisal), re-examine the wounds. **They do not re-weigh the cold
record. To revise the fact, they first REGAIN ACCESS.**

And note the CHANNEL: **技术性证据 — technical/forensic/physical evidence.** Not
credibility. Not demeanor.

## ⭐ THE DOCUMENTARY/CREDIBILITY SPLIT, IN MANDARIN
| | WEST | CHINA |
|---|---|---|
| channel that MOVES facts | **documents** the court can read (5/5) | **技术性证据** it can re-examine (重新鉴定) |
| channel that CANNOT | credibility / demeanor (0/14) | cold-record re-weighing |
| the operative rule | "clearly erroneous" (a standard of review) | **"未提交新的证据 → 予以确认"** (the access condition, stated outright) |

## THE VERDICT
**China does not refute A0. China states A0 more explicitly than any Western system.**
Western courts BURY the access principle inside a standard of review ("palpable and
overriding error", "l'appréciation souveraine", "kein Rechtsfehler"). The Chinese court
gives the REASON: *you submitted no new evidence, therefore the facts stand.*

**Decisive point: China HAD THE POWER TO DO OTHERWISE.** 全面审查 permits full fact
re-examination. A0 predicted that ACCESS — not doctrine — governs whether that power is
exercised. **It does.** This is A0's second instantiation, exactly as pre-registered:
same axiom, different institutional design, different surface behaviour.

**⚠ HONEST LIMITS:** n is small (1 blind-committed case + 1 SPP典型案例 collection).
These are SELECTED cases (指导性/典型案例), not a random sample. A full blind Chinese
register is still owed. What is established is the STRUCTURAL rule and the access
condition in the courts' own words — not a rate.

**FOREIGN REGISTER NOW: 11 cases / 6 systems (UK, AU, CA, FR, DE, CN) —
common law, civil law, AND socialist law. Fact-seam: 11/11.**
