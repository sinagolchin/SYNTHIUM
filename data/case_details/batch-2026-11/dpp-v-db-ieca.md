## Facts
Convicted of 35 indecent assaults on A.A. (younger sister of his then-girlfriend, later wife), 1982-87, and one on
her sister B.A., 1988-91. Historic allegations; defence theory at trial: allegations generated within the later
matrimonial dispute.

## Four grounds, all rejected, with the standard used
1. **Refusal to allow cross-examination about abuse by a different (deceased) man** — REJECTED. Statutory gate,
   s 3(2)(b) Criminal Law (Rape) Act 1981; "no sound basis on which any court applying that test could conclude"
   the question could meet it; the theory was "wild speculation". Legal test applied by the appeal court itself.
2. **No direction that complaint/disclosure evidence is not proof of the truth (hearsay)** — REJECTED. Not
   requisitioned at trial; Cronin (No. 2) test: point not raised at trial is entertained only for an oversight of
   substance causing fundamental injustice. Neither limb met; competent counsel might tactically "leave well enough
   alone"; risk the jury misused it was "remote" on the run of the trial. M.A. distinguished.
3. **Judge asking in jury's presence whether the defence would call evidence** — REJECTED. No rule of law against it;
   standard no-adverse-inference directions were given; "lacks any substance".
4. **Admission of C.A.'s evidence (prejudice vs probative value)** — REJECTED. Trial judge "correctly exercised his
   discretion"; evidence relevant to specific issues, not general disposition.

## Disposition: APPEAL AGAINST CONVICTION DISMISSED (verdicts not unsafe, trial not unsatisfactory).

## Seam reading
No fact-side attack at all: the jury's credibility verdict on A.A. was never directly challenged; every ground was
procedural/evidential law. The court reviewed the legal tests itself (s 3(2)(b); relevance) and deferred on
discretion (admission of C.A.) and on run-of-trial judgment. The "unsafe verdict" frame keeps fact-finding armored:
even an admitted legal omission (ground 2) did not move the case because its effect on the verdict was judged remote.
