# Commonwealth v. Goncalves, SJC-13880 (Mass. Supreme Judicial Court, 2026-10-06) — FULL TEXT VERIFIED
**Bench:** Budd, C.J., Gaziano, Kafker, Wendlandt, Georges, Dewar, Wolohojian, JJ. (opinion by Dewar, J.)
**Below:** Edgartown District Court, jury trial (Barnes, J.); direct appellate review granted.
**Source:** CourtListener opinion 11480363 (cluster 11012675), read in full (all 9 chunks, 52,823 chars, incl. Appendix model instruction).

## Facts
Defendant installed an always-on home camera high in a living-room corner, angled at the couch, next to a dark pipe
and a ceiling gap. A man he met on a dating app (whose profile said "I will never make videos") had a sexual encounter
with him on that couch. Two weeks later, when the man texted, defendant sent him a still of the two of them nude.
Confronted, he replied "Block u. Bye." At the station he laughed, "So I make porno." Convicted by jury of
(1) photographing an unsuspecting nude person, G.L. c. 272 §105(b), and (2) disseminating the image, §105(c).
Sentence: 90 days HOC, 10 to serve, rest suspended; 1 yr administrative probation.

## Claims and the standard the court actually used
1. **Sufficiency: willfulness (accident defense)** — REJECTED. Latimore standard: evidence viewed "in the light most
   favorable to the Commonwealth"; inferences need only be "reasonable and possible, not necessary or inescapable";
   "it is for the jury to determine where the truth lies." (deferential, fact-side)
2. **Sufficiency: intent to secretly conduct/hide** — REJECTED, same deferential standard.
3. **Jury instruction on willfulness (unpreserved)** — REJECTED. Reviewed for substantial risk of a miscarriage of
   justice; no error read as a whole. Court adds a model instruction (Appendix).
4. **Is sending the image only to the victim "disseminat[ing]" under §105(c)?** — ACCEPTED. "We review this question of
   statutory interpretation DE NOVO." Plain meaning (spreading), graduated penalty scheme (dissemination punished 2x),
   §31 definition expressly limited to other statutes, and rule of lenity → dissemination requires at least one third party.

## Disposition: §105(b) conviction AFFIRMED; §105(c) conviction REVERSED, judgment of not guilty to enter.

## Seam reading
Clean split inside one opinion: every fact-side attack (two sufficiency claims about what the defendant intended)
failed under the deferential standard; the one movement was a pure question of law reviewed de novo.
Consistent with W (when a case moves, the law moved). Note: the reversal is an acquittal on that count because the
law redefinition left no evidence of the element; the jury's fact findings were not disturbed.
