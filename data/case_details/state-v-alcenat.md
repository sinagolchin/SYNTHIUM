# State v. Alcenat, AC47150 (Conn. App. 2026-07-14) — FULL TEXT VERIFIED
**Judges:** Moll, Westbrook, Wilson, Js. (opinion by Moll, J.)
**Trial court:** Superior Court, JD of Hartford, K. Doyle, J. (jury trial)
**Full opinion:** storage.courtlistener.com/pdf/2026/07/14/state_v._alcenat.pdf (read in full)

## Facts
2:30 a.m., Manchester CT. Officer sees a lone running car in a Dollar General lot.
Car exits erratically — illegal left turn over a curb, lane swerve, parks half on the
sidewalk. Driver (Alcenat) confused, pinpoint pupils, smell of alcohol (one officer),
fails horizontal-gaze-nystagmus and walk-and-turn field sobriety tests, refuses
breath/urine test. Convicted by jury of OUI, §14-227a(a)(1). Sentenced to 6 months
suspended, 18 months probation, $500 fine, MADD victim impact panel.

## Five claims, all rejected — with the standard of review the court actually used
1. **Insufficient evidence** — REJECTED. "This court cannot substitute its own judgment
   for that of the jury." Cumulative force (erratic driving + failed tests + refusal +
   pinpoint pupils + alcohol smell) supports guilt.
2. **Motion to suppress (was he seized in the lot?)** — REJECTED. Fact finding reviewed
   for CLEAR ERROR; trial court's "not seized in the lot" finding was not clearly
   erroneous. Whether reasonable suspicion existed on the street = PLENARY review, and
   the stop was justified.
3. **Officer as HGN expert** — DECLINED (inadequately briefed on harm).
4. **No specific unanimity instruction (liquor vs drug vs both)** — REJECTED. The three
   are alternative MEANS of one element, not separate elements; general unanimity
   suffices. (Detailed statutory-construction analysis, plenary review.)
5. **MADD panel as probation condition (compelled speech)** — DECLINED (unpreserved,
   record inadequate — Golding prong 1).

## Disposition: AFFIRMED.
