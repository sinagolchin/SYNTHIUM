# Gleason v. Durden, 211 Conn. App. 416 (2022) — AC43738
**Judges:** Bright, C.J., Prescott, Alexander, Js. | **Filed:** 2022-03-29
**Trial court:** Superior Court, Fairfield JD, Hon. Michael Hartmere, judge trial referee

## Facts
Family real-estate dispute among siblings after their mother's death in 1973.
Plaintiff (John Gleason) alleged a "family cooperative agreement" governed the
siblings' inherited assets. Sued siblings Marcella, H, C and brother-in-law A for
unjust enrichment and breach of contract over the "Haverhill Road" property, where
defendants resided 1973/1985-present, paying mortgage (paid off 1992), taxes,
insurance, upkeep. Also involves a 2017 condominium purchase (Marcella contributed
$18,000).

## Procedural history
Trial: judgment in part for plaintiff. Defendants appealed; plaintiff cross-appealed.

## Held
1. Trial court ERRED granting the unjust-enrichment judgment: it relied on an
   agreement (linking the Haverhill Road transfer to a separate Newtown property
   sale) that the plaintiff's OWN COMPLAINT never alleged. Can't win on an unpled
   theory.
2. Plaintiff's cross-appeal: DECLINED FOR REVIEW — incomplete trial transcript
   provided, court cannot assess the claim.

## Disposition
**Reversed in part; judgment directed.**
