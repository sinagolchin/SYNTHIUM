# Copper City Gaming, Inc. v. Allen, 2026 MT 230 (Mont. Sept. 29, 2026)
Source: CourtListener op 11454075, full text read after commit. Campaign A id ca-copper-city-allen, grade blind.
Committed: pure_law / REVERSED. Actual: pure_law / REVERSED. Notch hit.
- Corporation sued a former shareholder's relative over $14,400 storage-lease payments; district court dismissed under 12(b)(6) on issue preclusion from a prior shareholder suit settled by stipulated dismissal (Special Master order waived "storage unit fees" disputes).
- Held: the record did not conclusively show an identical issue actually decided, adequate representation/privity, or full and fair opportunity; and the court resolved disputed inferences from outside records without Rule 12(d) conversion (Roaring Lion distinguished, Plouffe followed).
- Reversed and remanded; settlement's contractual effect left open.
