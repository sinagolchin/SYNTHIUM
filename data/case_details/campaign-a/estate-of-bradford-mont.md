# In re Estate of Bradford, 2026 MT 251 (Mont. Oct. 27, 2026)
Source: CourtListener op 11514123, full text read after commit. Campaign A id ca-estate-bradford, grade blind.
Committed: procedural / REVERSED. Actual: procedural / REVERSED. Notch hit.
- PR (daughter) removed the same day objectors filed, without hearing or reasons; later special administrator appointed and all accounts (incl. claimed nonprobate) frozen, again without hearing.
- Held: removal violated 72-3-526(1)-(2) (hearing and determination required); special administrator appointment failed 72-3-701(2) (no hearing, emergency not shown); blanket freeze was an abuse of discretion in scope. Standing: will-contest standing does not by itself give standing to remove for cause (Lawlor), but objectors may seek 72-3-304 interim protection.
- All three orders reversed; PR restored subject to formal-proceeding limits. No view on will validity. Procedure moved; no fact findings were made below to disturb.
