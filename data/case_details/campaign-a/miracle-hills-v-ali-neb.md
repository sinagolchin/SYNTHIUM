Facts: judgment creditor foreclosed its judgment lien on the debtor's undivided half interest in the family home held in joint tenancy with his wife. Summary judgment; decree ordered sale of the "Real Property" by the sheriff or a special master of the creditor's choice.

Holding: the homestead exemption did not apply because no application under 40-105 to 40-107 was filed; a homestead exists only on residence plus selection, so it is not a limit on judicial power. But plain error: the decree ordered sale of the whole residence though the lien attached only to the debtor's half, and it left the choice of seller to one party. Vacated and remanded.
