# YC Properties, LLC v. Connolly, 2026 MT 261 (Mont. Nov. 4, 2026)
Source: CourtListener op 11522243, full text read after commit. Campaign A id ca-yc-properties-connolly, grade blind.
Committed: pure_law / REVERSED. Actual: pure_law / REVERSED. Notch hit.
- Water Court granted SJ that wells claimed for "commercial beaver pens" were abandoned because later stock/irrigation use was unauthorized (no DNRC change approval), treating unauthorized use as the functional equivalent of intent to abandon.
- Held: Water Court may consider post-1973 conduct (affirmed on that issue), but "non-use" does not include use for another beneficial purpose as a matter of law; abandonment remains a question of fact requiring all circumstances. The 85-2-226 conclusive presumption applies only to failure to file the original claim.
- Reversed SJ, remanded. Law moved (lower court's new rule rejected); no fact finding was disturbed (SJ posture).
