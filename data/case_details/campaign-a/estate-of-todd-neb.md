# In re Estate of Todd, 322 Neb. 719 (Neb. Oct. 30, 2026, No. S-25-743)
Source: CourtListener opinion 11518004, full text read (blind protocol, case id ca-estate-todd). Papik, J.
Campaign A: grade blind; prior exposure case name only. Committed CLEAN_AFFIRM / pure_law. Actual CLEAN_AFFIRM / pure_law. Notch hit.

Posture: decedent's partner offered a 2021 two-page handwritten document, signed and dated by him but written in her hand, as his holographic will superseding a 1984 will. County court granted the son summary judgment.

Holding: a joint will is separate wills of each maker; in the decedent's estate only he can be the testator, and the material provisions were not in his handwriting, so the document is not his valid holographic will under Neb. Rev. Stat. 30-2328 (syllabus point 4). Alternative ground (no testamentary intent) not reached. Affirmed. (de novo, pure law, held)
