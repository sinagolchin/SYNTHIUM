# Colton v. Overlook at Fox Creek Subdivision Owners' Ass'n (Idaho Sup. Ct., Nov. 9, 2026, Docket 52603)
Source: CourtListener opinion 11525565, full text read (blind protocol, case id ca-colton-overlook). Moeller, J.; unanimous.
Campaign A: grade blind; prior exposure case name only. Committed CLEAN_AFFIRM / pure_law. Actual REVERSED / pure_law. Notch miss.

Posture: pro se neighbour sued HOA, developer and lot owners for declaration on who must plow/maintain an unplowed road segment. District court dismissed (no privity, so no standing), then granted HOA summary judgment on its counterclaim because "no evidence" the road lay within the subdivision; awarded fees.

Holdings:
1. Dismissal reversed: court failed to address the alternative equity/easement claim; under I.C. 10-1202 a person whose rights are "affected by" a contract may seek a declaration; notice pleading satisfied; standing adequately alleged. (de novo, pure law, moved)
2. Summary judgment reversed: as movant on its own counterclaim the HOA bore the burden to show the road lies outside the subdivision; the court shifted the burden prematurely. Record maps inconclusive, so a genuine fact issue exists. (de novo; record-absence finding disturbed)
3. Fee awards vacated; no appellate fees; costs to Colton.
