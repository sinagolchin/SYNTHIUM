# Ellman v. Charron (Idaho Sup. Ct., Oct 20 2026), Campaign A case ca-ellman-charron

Facts: grandfather sought a writ of mandate in Ada County compelling the Department of Health and Welfare to issue an APA final order on its permanency placement recommendation in a Kootenai County CPA case. District court denied (no subject matter jurisdiction, no standing, no mandatory duty).

Holding: affirmed on subject matter jurisdiction. The CPA gives the CPA court exclusive jurisdiction over review of the placement recommendation (I.C. 16-1603, 16-1622, 16-1629). Due process claim fails: no protected interest in placement. The court also held that its permissive-intervention holding in Roe (2000) no longer applies after the 2013 CPA amendments and 16-1613, so the grandfather may move to intervene in the CPA case at that court's discretion.
