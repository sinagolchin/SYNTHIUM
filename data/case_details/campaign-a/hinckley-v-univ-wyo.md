- Pro se former employee; Rule 12(b)(6) dismissal without prejudice. Court declined summary affirmance for brief defects and reached the merits.
- WGCA bars tort damages (fraud, IIED, public-policy wrongful termination); it does NOT bar injunctive relief (1-39-116). Court corrected the University's broader position but the result held.
- Public-policy wrongful termination fails because WFEPA gives an adequate remedy (incl. reinstatement).
- WFEPA claims must reach court as a W.R.A.P. 12 petition for review, not a Rule 3 complaint; district court properly declined jurisdiction.
- Rule 59(e) denial: no abuse of discretion.
